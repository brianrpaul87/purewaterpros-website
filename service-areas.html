Communities served

Serving Greater Victoria, the West Shore, Sooke and the Saanich Peninsula.

Pure Water Pros provides scheduled residential and light-commercial water-treatment service across Greater Victoria, including Victoria, Langford and the West Shore, the Saanich Peninsula, Sooke and nearby communities. Availability depends on the type of work, travel requirements and appointment capacity.

Victoria and the urban core

Victoria, Saanich, Oak Bay and Esquimalt, including municipal-water filtration, reverse osmosis and existing-equipment service.

Langford and the West Shore

Langford, Colwood, View Royal, Highlands and Metchosin, including municipal-water and private-well treatment.

The Saanich Peninsula

Central Saanich, North Saanich and Sidney, with treatment selected around water source, flow, testing and existing equipment.

Sooke and western communities

Scheduled service for Sooke and nearby communities, with particular focus on private-well filtration, iron and sulfur treatment, UV, oxidation and low-yield storage where testing and site conditions support it.

Services across the region

Water treatment matched to the source and the problem.

Municipal-water filtration

Whole-home carbon, sediment filtration and drinking-water treatment for chlorine, taste and odour concerns.
