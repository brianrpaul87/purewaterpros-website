## Approved cadence
One finalized article every 2 days, in this order:
1. UV / power outage
2. CRD water-quality results explainer
3. Diagnose before buying more treatment equipment
4. Iron types in well water
5. UV annual service
6. Water-softener salt bridge

Routine educational articles within existing Pure Water Pros guidance do not require individual owner approval.

Stop for owner review only if the content introduces or changes:
- pricing, warranties, discounts, offers, or company policy;
- sensitive health/regulatory claims;
- treatment recommendations materially inconsistent with existing company guidance;
- unverified local water-quality claims.
