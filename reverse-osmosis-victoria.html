Practical system planning

Good RO is about verified performance—not just filter changes.

A reverse-osmosis system needs adequate pressure, appropriate pretreatment, a healthy membrane and proper sanitizing. Performance should be checked with feed and product-water readings rather than judged by taste alone.

    Review the goal

RO rental questions

What to know before renting a reverse-osmosis system.

Is the rental rent-to-own?

No. The standard Pure Water Pros rental is a straightforward equipment rental. Pure Water Pros retains ownership of the rental system.

How long is the term?

The initial rental term is 24 months. After that, the agreement continues month-to-month.

Are filters included?

Routine filter service is separate from the monthly rental: $59 service call plus the filters required for the system.

What about the RO membrane?

We verify membrane performance with feed and product-water readings. Replacement is recommended when testing supports it.

What is included with installation?

Standard installation includes a dedicated faucet, normal drilling where appropriate, connections, storage tank, leak-detection tray, commissioning and TDS verification.

What if my installation is unusual?

Non-standard plumbing, access or installation requirements are identified first and quoted before additional work is authorized.
